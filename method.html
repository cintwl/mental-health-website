PROJECT PROCESS

Method + AI

Behind Beyond Shyness: the thinking, research and development process, and the role of the AI Buddy as a support tool.

PROJECT PURPOSE

Why this website?

Beyond Shyness is designed as an educational and interactive resource to help university students understand social anxiety. I wanted to create a space that goes beyond traditional text-heavy articles, using evidence-informed concepts, lived-experience storytelling, and interactive self-reflection to encourage help-seeking behavior.

APPROACH

How the content is organised

The website brings together psychological concepts, research themes, a fictional student story, self-reflection activities and signposting to support resources. The visual identity uses a calming "Artistic Editorial" style to ensure the topic feels approachable, non-judgmental, and safe to explore at one's own pace.

THE DEVELOPMENT JOURNEY

How Beyond Shyness Took Shape

01Brainstorming & directionDefining the topic and audience

The project begins with a focus on social anxiety among university students. I wanted to avoid clinical jargon and instead build an empathetic, interactive experience specifically tailored to the Hong Kong campus context.

02Research & content organisationTurning information into an understandable journey

I researched leading psychological models (like Clark & Wells' cognitive model), the Mini-SPIN screening tool, and Hong Kong's specific mental health landscape. This data was then organised into a narrative flow: Understanding → Evidence → Self-Check → Story → Help.

03Visual design & InteractionBuilding the Artistic Editorial identity

Using warm neutral space, dusty blue, and muted teal, I designed an interface that feels calm. Interactive elements—like the animated charts and the social anxiety cycle—were built to keep visitors engaged without overwhelming them.

04Website developmentStructuring pages and interactions

HTML provides the structure, CSS shapes the visual identity, and JavaScript powers the interactive experiences (the self-check scoring, story branching, and comfort planner).

MEET MY AI BUDDY

How AI supported the project

For this project, I utilized an AI assistant (like Word Buddy and other generative AI tools) as a "co-pilot." The AI did not replace my creative vision or academic judgement; rather, it acted as an accelerator for my ideas, a coding tutor, and a structural editor.

AI Buddy (The Assistant)

  • Coding & Debugging: Helped write the HTML/CSS for the responsive layout and fixed complex JavaScript bugs (like the scroll-spy navigation and score calculation).
  • Design Refinement: Suggested UI improvements, such as replacing underlines with "pillow" navigation styles and adding scroll-reveal animations.
  • Content Drafting: Assisted in rephrasing clinical concepts into more approachable, empathetic language for the website sections.
  • Structure Generation: Provided the initial scaffolding for the interactive cards and timeline layouts.

Project Creator (Me)

  • Vision & Direction: Decided the core message: "Social anxiety is more than shyness." Chose the specific colors, fonts, and overall vibe.
  • Academic Accuracy: Selected the specific research data, wrote the Mini-SPIN questions, and verified the Hong Kong support links.
  • Storytelling: Conceptualized "Mei's Story" and wrote the narrative for the branching scenarios.
  • Quality Control: Tested every button, adjusted the spacing, and ensured the final website felt human, warm, and safe.

This project demonstrates how human empathy and AI technical capabilities can collaborate to create accessible mental health resources. The AI handled the "how" (the code), allowing me to focus deeply on the "why" and "what" (the message and the people).
